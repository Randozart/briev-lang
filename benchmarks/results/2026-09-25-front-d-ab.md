## Verdict

**The deferred-region emitter does not retire.** It is load-bearing
twice over on the composite shape:

1. Performance: its 1024-thread / 32-warp-slice layout gives ~26× over
   the plain path's 64-thread lane-reduction lowering on NKV=4096.
2. History: until `cc1254d4` it was also the ONLY correct lane for the
   shape (the plain path miscompiled store loops).

The knob stays at `1` (config/ir-lowering.dbvl:115, baked into brievc
via `include_str!`). Retirement becomes discussable only when the
GENERAL path grows its own multi-warp j-slicing with shared-memory
merge for block-per-workitem kernels — i.e. re-deriving the deferred
emitter's structure as general machinery (which is the M-ladder work in
`docs/plans/2026-09-20-gpu-dialect-beyond-cuda.md`, not a knob flip).
